world_countries.geojson and world_cities.geojson are Natural Earth vector data
(ne_110m_admin_0_countries and ne_110m_populated_places_simple), in the public
domain. world_detail.bin is built from Natural Earth 10m data
(ne_10m_admin_0_countries, ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces and
ne_10m_populated_places_simple) by tools/world_data/build_world_data.py. Source:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com via https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector .
Made with Natural Earth.
